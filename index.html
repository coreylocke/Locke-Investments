What we do

Locke Investments operates within e-commerce retail and wholesale procurement, sourcing legitimate consumer inventory through established commercial supply relationships.

E-Commerce Retail

Online retail operations

We develop and operate online retail channels designed to serve U.S. consumers across selected product categories.

Wholesale Purchasing

Legitimate inventory sourcing

We purchase inventory from manufacturers, authorized distributors and established wholesale suppliers.

We maintain appropriate purchasing records and supplier documentation for inventory acquired for resale.

Commerce Operations

Structured operations

We manage product evaluation, purchasing, inventory, fulfillment coordination, pricing and marketplace compliance through structured internal processes.

How products enter our portfolio

Every purchasing opportunity moves through a structured evaluation process before inventory is acquired.

01

Market Evaluation

Evaluate consumer demand, sales history, marketplace conditions, competition, pricing behavior, category restrictions, marketplace eligibility and brand limitations.

02

Supplier Verification

Evaluate business legitimacy, inventory authenticity, invoice quality, product availability, shipping capability, account requirements, returns procedures, marketplace restrictions and brand authorization when applicable.

03

Cost & Opportunity Analysis

Evaluate unit cost, freight, marketplace fees, fulfillment costs, expected returns, inventory velocity, competitive pricing, margin requirements, restock potential and total landed cost.

04

Purchase & Monitoring

Approved opportunities move into purchasing, inventory receiving, 3PL coordination, pricing monitoring, marketplace monitoring, replenishment analysis and supplier relationship management.
