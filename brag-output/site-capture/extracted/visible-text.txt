[h2] What we do
[p] Locke Investments operates within wholesale procurement and multichannel retail, sourcing legitimate consumer inventory through established commercial supply relationships.
[b] Wholesale Procurement
[h3] Strategic sourcing operations
[p] We source inventory through established commercial supply relationships and distribute across approved retail channels serving U.S. consumers.
[p] Product distribution occurs through approved marketplaces, direct retail properties and other channels as they become active.
[b] Wholesale Purchasing
[h3] Legitimate inventory sourcing
[p] We purchase inventory from manufacturers, authorized distributors and established wholesale suppliers.
[p] We maintain appropriate purchasing records and supplier documentation for inventory acquired for resale.
[b] Commerce Operations
[h3] Structured operations
[p] We manage product evaluation, purchasing, inventory, fulfillment coordination, pricing and marketplace compliance through structured internal processes.
[h2] How products enter our portfolio
[p] Every purchasing opportunity moves through a structured evaluation process before inventory is acquired.
[h3] Market Evaluation
[p] Evaluate consumer demand, sales history, marketplace conditions, competition, pricing behavior, category restrictions, marketplace eligibility and brand limitations.
[h3] Supplier Verification
[p] Evaluate business legitimacy, inventory authenticity, invoice quality, product availability, shipping capability, account requirements, returns procedures, marketplace restrictions and brand authorization when applicable.
[h3] Cost & Opportunity Analysis
[p] Evaluate unit cost, freight, marketplace fees, fulfillment costs, expected returns, inventory velocity, competitive pricing, margin requirements, restock potential and total landed cost.
[h3] Purchase & Monitoring
[p] Approved opportunities move into purchasing, inventory receiving, 3PL coordination, pricing monitoring, marketplace monitoring, replenishment analysis and supplier relationship management.
