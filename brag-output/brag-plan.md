# Brag Plan: Locke Investments LLC

## What is this app?
Locke Investments LLC is a Michigan-based wholesale procurement and multichannel retail company — sourcing consumer products through manufacturers, authorized distributors and wholesale suppliers, with documented inventory and structured operations.

## The angle
A serious, polished B2B film: "Better sourcing. Stronger commerce." The video traces the supply chain route diagram from the site — Suppliers → Locke Investments → 3PL Infrastructure → Retail Channels — and lands on the wholesale account invitation. Quiet confidence, gold-on-navy, sharp rectangles. This is a company that wants suppliers to trust it, so the video sells legitimacy and structure.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Better sourcing." in gold-on-navy display type with the site's sharp 2px-corner aesthetic, tag "WHOLESALE PROCUREMENT · MULTICHANNEL RETAIL" above it. The word "sourcing" carries the promise.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The route diagram animating: 4 nodes appear one by one (SUPPLIERS → LOCKE INVESTMENTS → 3PL INFRASTRUCTURE → RETAIL CHANNELS), with an animated flow line drawing between them. This is the product's core concept, straight from the site's hero SVG.
- "Stronger commerce." headline lands as the flow completes.
- Sourcing categories pulse in as chips (Beauty, Home & Kitchen, Tools, Pet, Sports, Office...) signaling portfolio breadth — 15 categories from the site.
- The verification list ticks up: "Michigan LLC Documentation · EIN & W-9 · 3PL Receiving Infrastructure · Electronic Purchasing Workflows" — the legitimacy proof points.

## Outro / punchline
Gold block from the site: "Open for wholesale accounts. Let's build a long-term purchasing relationship." + the Locke Investments wordmark. Ends on the invitation.

## User flow worth showing
None — landing-page-only corporate site. Rely on the route diagram (the strongest visual) + category chips + verification list.

- Creative direction: quiet premium B2B supply-chain film
- Interpretation: Few scenes, longer holds, generous negative space. Type is confident and restrained. Motion is structural (flow lines, node reveals) rather than playful. Gold is the only accent.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 2.5s
Navy background. Gold status-bar strip at top (from site). Top-left small gold label "LOCKE INVESTMENTS LLC". Center: "Better sourcing." — white weight-800 Manrope, settled for 1.5s. Tag above reads "WHOLESALE PROCUREMENT · MULTICHANNEL RETAIL".
Sequential/interaction: none — single type reveal
Audio intent: confident, quiet opening; music bed starts
Transition mood: hard cut with gold flash → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The Route — 6s
The site's route diagram reconstructs: node 1 SUPPLIERS (MANUFACTURERS · DISTRIBUTORS) slides in at 0.4s, gold flow line draws down, node 2 LOCKE INVESTMENTS LLC (PURCHASING · ANALYSIS · COMPLIANCE) at 2.0s, flow line draws, node 3 3PL INFRASTRUCTURE (RECEIVING · STORAGE · FULFILLMENT) at 3.4s, flow line draws, node 4 RETAIL CHANNELS → CONSUMERS at 4.8s. Line drawing animates node-to-node.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 nodes + 3 flow lines appear in strict sequence, each with a soft accent
Audio intent: building — each node is a step forward
Audio-coupled idea: node-arrival drops, line-draw whooshes (subtle)
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Stronger Commerce — 3.5s
Route diagram recedes to the upper third (smaller, still visible). Center headline slams in white: "Stronger commerce." with "commerce." in gold. Sub-line: "Multichannel retail · documented inventory · structured operations."
Sequential/interaction: yes — headline, then gold word, then sub-line in quick succession
Audio intent: the payoff moment — music swells, one bell on the gold word
Audio-coupled idea: single impactBell on "Stronger commerce." landing
Transition mood: clean wipe → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Categories & Verification — 4.5s
Two stacked panels: left panel "WHAT WE SOURCE" — 6 category chips pop in one by one (Beauty & Personal Care, Home & Kitchen, Pet Supplies, Tools & Home Improvement, Sports & Outdoors, Office Products). Right panel "WHAT SUPPLIERS GET" — checklist rows tick in: Michigan LLC Documentation, EIN & W-9, 3PL Receiving, Electronic Workflows.
Sequential/interaction: yes — 6 chips and 4 checklist rows reveal in sequence with light ticks
Audio intent: credible, structured — ticks like a printer confirming boxes
Audio-coupled idea: chip ticks (accent first + last only)
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Outro — 3s
Gold block (site's .gold-block): "Open for wholesale accounts — Let's build a long-term purchasing relationship." CTA line + white wordmark "LOCKE INVESTMENTS LLC" with gold cargo-ship mark. Fade out.
Sequential/interaction: yes — gold block in, headline, then logo
Audio intent: warm close, music fades under the logo, one soft payoff
Audio-coupled idea: final impactSoft + logo settle
Transition mood: fade to end
